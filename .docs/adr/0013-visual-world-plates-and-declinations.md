# Visual world: Plates and Declinations

The site's visual world is an observatory plate atlas: near-black plate ground, ruled catalogue columns, engraved scale rails, tabular figures, and photographic plates as the model for how content sits on the page. It was chosen through the impeccable direction round (direction seed `07e77401`, mode read), and it replaces the earlier notion that a style reference would be adopted wholesale.

Chosen card: `model-pick` (my top-ranked grounded candidate, which the roll had set aside). The roll's assigned direction was `assigned` (Closed Stacks, a library card catalogue), kept in the hand as an alternate rather than built.

Palette, all from the pinned Cosmic Midnight ambient plus the plate's own black:

- plate `#05070c` ground

## Considered Options

- Closed Stacks (card catalogue) - the roll's assignment; full card, not built.
- Provenance Ribbon (museum custody) - competitive: provenance is already the product's own word (origin, promote, previous).
- Timetable Slide Rack - competitive: one type size, rank by weight, case and rule; a tick rail for long runs.
- Two-Ink One-Sheet (noir duotone) - competitive: the most cinematic hand, and the closest to monochrome editorial at full commitment.
- Cyclorama Dawn, Depot Destination Blind, Cutting Bench Rail - declined; each donated one discipline, recorded below.

## Consequences

- Three disciplines were donated to the built world and are part of the contract: one continuous field of light with no section boxes (cyclorama), motion as one mechanical step that overshoots and settles with nothing gliding or fading (destination blind), and state carried as a mark rather than a hue (cutting bench).
- The home composition was locked separately as Plate Stack (surface seed `b7cd988d`) in the surface brief `.impeccable/surfaces/src-pages-index-astro.md`.
- Execution path is code-led: no image generation exists in this environment, so no comp is owed, and the ambition is carried by the direction contract's first viewport and its named signature interaction.
- `DESIGN.md` is written at the finish from the built world, not before it.
- Type was decided in a specimen pass, not by default: Spectral (`400`/`600`) for reading and Archivo Narrow for labels and data. Source Serif 4, Newsreader, Literata and EB Garamond were rendered on the real page at real sizes and rejected; the specimens survive under `.impeccable/review/specimen/`.
