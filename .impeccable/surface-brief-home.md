# Home

**Scope.** The site root (`/`). Mode: Read.

**Audience and job.** A first-time technical reader, and the author re-reading his own corpus. Within one viewport they should know whose site this is, what it is about, and what the ten most recent articles are. Agents read the same material as raw markdown and through the catalog.

**Content.** `ABOUT.md` at the vault root supplies the note. The list is the ten newest published Articles. The three category links (Articles, Projects, Logs) live in the rule band. Projects and Logs never appear on this page.

**Constraints.** Zero JavaScript; the ~100ms client render budget holds. Contrast and semantics follow the WCAG 2.2 AA target. The publication boundary (registry-listed categories only) is enforced by the loader, identically here and in CI.

**Chosen direction.** Plate Stack, surface seed `b7cd988d`, position 7 of the grounded list. World: Plates and Declinations, direction seed `07e77401`, chosen card `model-pick`.

**Memorable moment.** The stack's paper edges: ten plates in one centred column, the observer's note resting on top, the newest plate carrying the single ember mark.

**Unresolved.** The typefaces (provisional pass in place, confirm at the type pass); the mark vocabulary for the article rows; the dressing of the four content surfaces; the article reading page.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The home is one observing log: a note on top of a stack of ten dated plates, and the same notes remain readable as raw material by agents. It refuses the category's default arrangement, an avatar beside a paragraph above a mixed feed of everything.

**OWN-WORLD.** Plate black `#05070c` ground, hairline rules in `#1b2230`, bone ink `#e8eef7` for text, star blue `#9fb6d9` for secondary ink, electric iris `#5683da` for the live link and the current mark, ember `#ff8964` held to exactly one mark per view. Component language: rule bands, ruled rows, tabular figures, engraved scale caps, paper edges stacked with real offsets. Serif for reading, condensed grotesque for labels and data.

**STORY.** The visitor understands this is a working observatory of notes rather than a blog, sees a name and a note, and reads down a dated stack of ten articles. Category links are in the rule band; Projects and Logs are elsewhere.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** A full-width rule band at the top: site name left, category links right, hairline above and below. Directly beneath it the observer's note, measure 65ch. Then the stack: ten ruled rows, each carrying a tabular date, a title and a magnitude mark, the newest marked in ember. No hero image, no cards, no boxes, no kicker.

**FORM.** Plate Stack, position 7, surface seed `b7cd988d`; world from direction seed `07e77401`, card `model-pick`.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
